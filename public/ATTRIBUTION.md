# Mars texture attribution

\`mars-nasa.jpg\` is a temporary 1440 × 720 equirectangular Mars texture supplied from NASA's 3D resources. It is not original OWN MARS artwork and is not represented as such in the product.

Production still requires a separately licensed, photorealistic 8192 × 4096 (2:1) equirectangular texture, delivered as an optimized WebP with documented source and rights. Do not upsample this temporary asset and describe it as production quality.
